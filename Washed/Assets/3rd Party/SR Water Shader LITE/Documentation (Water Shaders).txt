Lite version of our asset "SR Water shader". This pack contains many Animated water Shaders/Materials for games. 
All materials are highly customizable too. Adjust the properties and create your own variation easily.

What's New in the Full Version?
Interactive Water: Generates foam around the edges of objects when they are placed in water.
More Water Variations: Explore additional water styles and variations.

Note: This asset uses Depth texture and Opaque texture (Quick Beginner friendly Guide included).

--------------- Water Shader Properties ---------------

Pattern Color : Changes the color of the secondary water pattern texture.

Water Depth : Controls the blend between shallow and deep water.

Refraction Strength : Controls the amount of water distortion.

Smoothness : Controls how glossy the water surface appears.
